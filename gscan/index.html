Scan every page of your work into one clean PDF. Free, with no ads.

Getting a great scan

  • Lay the page on a plain, darker table and hold your phone close above it.
  • Hold still — GScan snaps by itself and shows “Page saved ✓”. Then put the next page down.
  • If the edges can’t be found (a crumpled page, a whole whiteboard), tap the shutter button.
  • For whiteboards, step a little to the side so ceiling-light glare isn’t on the writing.

Common questions

Do I need to make one PDF per page?
No. Scan all your pages in one go — they all go into one PDF.

Can I fix a page afterwards?
Yes. Tap a page to turn it, change its look (Auto, B&W, Color), adjust its corners, retake it, or delete it. Hold and drag pages to reorder them.

How is my PDF named?
Last_First_Course_Assignment_Date.pdf — using the name you entered (optional) and the course and assignment you type when making the PDF.

How do I send it?
After “Create PDF”, tap “Send or Save PDF” to email it, AirDrop it, or save it to Files to upload.

Where are my scans stored?
Only on your iPhone, until you share the PDF. “Start Over” deletes them.

Something went wrong?

Open the page in GScan and tap the speech-bubble (!) button, or go to Settings → Report a Problem. You’ll see the email and its attachments before anything is sent.
