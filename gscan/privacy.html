GScan Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

GScan is a free app, with no ads, for scanning written schoolwork into a PDF. It is designed to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. GScan has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet on its own. The only way anything reaches us is if you choose to send a problem report (see below).

Your scans

Pages you scan are processed and stored only on your iPhone. They leave your phone only when you choose to share or save the PDF, for example by Mail, AirDrop or Save to Files. Tapping “Start Over” or “Start a New Scan” deletes the pages from the app.

Your name

Entering your name is optional. If you do, it is stored only on your iPhone and is used to name your PDF files. You can change or remove it at any time in Settings.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan pages while you are scanning.

Problem reports

If you tap “Report a Problem”, GScan prepares an email for you to review. You choose what to attach: the original photo of a page, the finished page, and technical details (your iPhone model, iOS version, the page’s settings, and a short log of what the scanner did — no names or images). The email is sent from your own email account, only when you press Send, to the address below. Photos may show your name or work; we use reports only to fix problems in GScan, never share them, and delete them once the problem is resolved.

The short technical log is kept only on your iPhone and is trimmed automatically. It leaves your phone only if you attach it to a report.

Children

GScan does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
