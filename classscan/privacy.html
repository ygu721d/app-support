ClassScan Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

ClassScan is a free app, with no ads, for scanning written schoolwork into a PDF. It is designed to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. ClassScan has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet on its own. The only way anything reaches us is if you choose to send a problem report (see below).

Your scans

Pages you scan are processed and stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They leave your device only when you choose to share or save the PDF, for example by Mail, AirDrop or Save to Files. Tapping “Start Over” or “Start a New Scan” deletes the pages from the app.

Your name

Entering your name is optional. If you do, it is stored only on your device and is used to name your PDF files. You can change or remove it at any time in Settings.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan pages while you are scanning.

Problem reports

If you tap “Report a Problem”, ClassScan prepares an email for you to review. You choose what to attach: the original photo of a page, the finished page, and technical details (your device model, system version, the page’s settings, and a short log of what the scanner did — no names or images). The email is sent from your own email account, only when you press Send, to the address below. Photos may show your name or work; we use reports only to fix problems in ClassScan, never share them, and delete them once the problem is resolved. To have a report you sent deleted sooner, email the address below and we will delete it.

The short technical log is kept only on your device, is not included in backups, and is trimmed automatically. It leaves your device only if you attach it to a report.

Children

ClassScan does not ask for anyone’s age and collects nothing automatically, from children or anyone else. A problem report is the only thing that can reach us, and only when the person using ClassScan chooses to send it. If you are a parent or guardian and want a report from your child deleted, email the address below and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
