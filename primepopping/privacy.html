Prime Popping Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Prime Popping is a rhythm game about prime numbers. It is designed to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Prime Popping has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no server of its own to connect to. We never see how you play.

Your progress

Your ladder, scores, medals, streak and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in your own iCloud account, so a new or restored iPhone can pick up where you left off. Apple keeps it in your account; we cannot see it.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you are signed in, your best scores and achievements are sent to Apple’s Game Center under your Game Center profile, and the game shows your friends’ best scores beside yours. Game Center is Apple’s service and is covered by Apple’s privacy policy; we receive nothing from it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details; the game only learns which items you own.

Reminders and the widget

Streak reminders are optional notifications scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere. The home-screen widget reads your streak from the game on the same iPhone.

Challenges

If you send a friend a challenge, the game prepares a link with the song, the chart and your score. It goes only where you choose to send it, for example by Messages or Mail.

Children

Prime Popping does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
