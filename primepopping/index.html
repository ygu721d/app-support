Prime Popping Support

A rhythm game about prime numbers. Free to try.

How to play

  • Pop: tap a prime as it lands on the line, and it joins your orbit.
  • Multiply: tap the orbit, and its primes fly into the falling numbers.
  • Factorize: swipe a composite into the forge, then tap the forge on the beat until only primes are left.

The Game Guide in the app (the ? on the menu) explains every mechanic as it arrives.

Common questions

What is free?
The demo: 2, 3 and 5, two songs, every difficulty. The Spiral opens the full journey; The Outer Arm, Deep Field and The Thousand follow in order.

How do I get my purchases on another iPhone?
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. The four ladders and Autoplay can also be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

What are the Seven-Day Streak and the Assist Refill?
The Seven-Day Streak adds seven days to your daily streak. The Assist Refill brings today’s assists back to full once one has run out.

Is my progress backed up?
Yes, in your own iCloud account when you are signed in to iCloud. A new or restored iPhone picks it up.

How do I sign in to Game Center?
Finish a song and tap MENU, or tap the Game Center icon at the bottom of the menu.

The timing feels off with headphones.
Run the Tap test in Settings. The game also allows for a Bluetooth headset’s delay on its own.

Email 51-tax-lune@icloud.com with your iPhone model and what happened.
