Prime Popping Music and Font Rights

Last updated October 24, 2026

This page documents the rights to the third-party material in Prime Popping: the music, which the developer generated on Suno under a paid plan, and three fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Everything else in the game (code, art, sounds and text) is the developer’s own work.

Music

Every song in the game, including the menu music and the three songs of Song Pack 1, was generated by the developer (Yu Gu) on Suno. The developer holds a Suno Pro subscription (annual plan, August 17, 2026 to August 17, 2027), and every clip below was created and downloaded within that paid period.

Under Suno’s Terms of Service (effective September 3, 2026):

  • Pro and Premier Accounts: Suno assigns to a Pro or Premier subscriber all of its right, title and interest in the output generated from that subscriber’s submissions.
  • Permitted Commercial Use: output may be used commercially once it has been downloaded through Suno within the plan’s download allowance. Every game file is such a download (an MP3 export whose ID3 comment carries the clip id below); none is a recording or stream rip.

Suno’s own record for each clip (the ownership_reason field of its clip data) reads subscribed: made while the account was on a paid plan. Clips marked “cover” are Suno covers or remixes whose source is another clip generated by the same developer on the same account; no third-party material was uploaded or sampled.

Fonts

The game’s typefaces are Orbitron, Oxanium and Unica One, each distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence text of each is shipped inside the app bundle (Orbitron-OFL.txt, Oxanium-OFL.txt, UnicaOne-OFL.txt).
